Beyond what you can see

the universe is bigger than you think

The universe was born roughly 13.8 billion years ago in an event we call the Big Bang. But that wasn't an explosion in space—it was an explosion of space itself, expanding from a point smaller than an atom. In the first fraction of a second, it inflated to cosmic scales faster than the speed of light.
